Discussion

Trade-offs & open questions

Known trade-offs

Hosted embed / rerank (Cohere)

The physician's query can leave our trust boundary. The corpus isn't PHI, but a query may carry patient specifics — the same concern behind self-hosting Langfuse. Self-host the reranker for zero egress.

Writes go through the OpenEMR module

We save to the chart the source document plus the facts extracted from it (e.g. lab values as FHIR Observations), each linked to its source. Routing writes through the module — not the sidecar — keeps integrity & audit.
